Privacy Policy

Business Compliance Tracker. Last updated November 4, 2026.

Business Compliance Tracker is a private tool used by a small team to keep track of business permits, their issuance and expiry dates, and scanned copies of those permits. It is not offered to the general public.

What we collect

  • Sign-in details. When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address so we can check that you are on the team list.
  • Permit records. The information team members enter, such as permit types, properties, dates, priority and stage.
  • Scanned copies. Image or PDF files that team members upload to attach to a permit.

How it is used and stored

Sign-in and permit records are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore). Uploaded scans are stored in a single Google Drive folder owned by the account that runs this tracker. The app uses Google's limited drive.file permission, which only lets it work with files and folders that the app itself created. It cannot see or change any other files in that Google Drive.

The information is used only to run the tracker for the team. We do not sell it, share it for advertising, or give it to third parties, except for the Google services described above that host it.

Who can see it

Only people whose email addresses have been added to the team list can sign in and view permit records. Each uploaded scan is available through a link that is shown only to signed-in team members.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept for as long as the team needs it. Removing a scan or deleting a permit in the app also deletes the related file from Google Drive. To have your own information removed, contact us at the address below.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to businesscompliance180@gmail.com.
